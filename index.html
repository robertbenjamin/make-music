The CAM System

Finishing music is a sequencing problem. Create, Arrange and Mix each need a different mindset, so do them one at a time, in two phases each, and cross a checkpoint before you move on.

The CAM System

Hatched = done · green = checkpoints

One mindset at a time

Creating, arranging and mixing think in different ways. Swapping between them causes most stuck tracks.

Order, not a formula

Nothing new to do. It reorders what you already do, the way a cook sources, then cooks, then plates.

Checkpoints keep you moving

Each stage ends at a green marker, so you always know when it's finished and don't drift back.

Aim for imperfectly finished

Perfect is the wrong target at every stage. Imperfect is what gets finished.

Make it yours

  1. Tailor your own process. Your genre, strengths, weaknesses and gaps decide the details inside each phase.
  2. Test and refine. When a problem keeps coming back, change the process so it can't.
  3. Prove it with evidence. Finished tracks build the confidence that silences self-doubt.
